## 👨‍💻 About me

I'm a final-year B.Tech student in **Computer Science & Engineering (AI & ML)** at Future Institute of Technology, Kolkata. I build intelligent systems at the intersection of **healthcare, agentic AI and data privacy**: from LSTM-based ICU mortality prediction to multi-agent clinical triage on IBM Watsonx.ai.

- 🏥 Currently exploring **AI for clinical triage and Explainable AI** for medical diagnostics
- 🤖 Building **multi-agent RAG pipelines** with LangChain, ChromaDB and FAISS
- 🔐 Interested in **privacy-preserving AI** (DPDP Act 2023 compliance, PII masking before LLM calls)
- 🌍 Building multilingual AI that works in **English, Hindi, Bengali & Hinglish**
- 🏆 **8th out of 200 teams** at Glacier Hack 2025

## 🚀 Featured projects

| Project | What it does | Stack |
|---|---|---|
| 🩺 [**Sanjeevani AI**](https://github.com/snehashish27/agentic-health-checker) | Multi-agent clinical triage platform on IBM Watsonx.ai with symptom extraction, guideline retrieval (WHO & govt. sources), a hospital finder, a vitals analyzer and a lab report explainer. Multilingual. | Watsonx.ai · ChromaDB · RAG |
| 🔐 [**Data Compliance & Risk Dashboard**](https://github.com/snehashish27/compliance-assistant) | Privacy-first compliance platform. Detects Aadhaar, PAN, email, phone and card numbers, and answers questions over masked documents so raw PII never reaches an LLM. DPDP Act aligned reports. | FastAPI · spaCy · FAISS · LangChain · Gemini |
| ❤️ [**ICU Mortality Prediction**](https://github.com/snehashish27/Survival-Prediction) | 2-layer stacked LSTM predicting in-hospital mortality from 41 physiological time-series inputs. **0.7943 AUC-ROC** on PhysioNet 2012. | PyTorch · AdamW · Class-weighted loss |
| 🦟 [**Malaria Detection (Malcnn)**](https://github.com/snehashish27/Malcnn) | Custom CNN trained on 27,000 blood smear images to detect parasitized cells, with augmentation and a Streamlit app. | TensorFlow · Keras · OpenCV · Streamlit |
| 🌬️ [**Wind Turbine Failure Prediction**](https://github.com/snehashish27/Wind-Turbine-Failure-Prediction-Using-Multilayer-Perceptron) | Predictive maintenance MLP on sensor telemetry with anomaly detection (Edunet Foundation × Shell Foundation). | Scikit-Learn · Keras · MLP |
